# Site Architecture

## Public Routes
- `/` - Home
- `/about` - About Us
- `/services` - Services
- `/gallery` - Gallery
- `/contact` - Contact Us

## Protected Administration Routes
- `/admin/login` - Admin Login
- `/admin` - Admin Dashboard
- `/admin/services` - Services Management
- `/admin/gallery` - Gallery Management

## Shared Components
- **Header/Navigation**: Consistent navigation system with active-page indicators and responsive mobile navigation.
- **Footer**: A polished footer with contact information, quick links, and business hours/details.
- **Breadcrumbs**: Meaningful breadcrumbs on deep pages to aid navigation.
- **Forms**: Reusable, accessible form components (inputs, textareas, buttons) with built-in validation states.

## Page Structures
- **Home**: High-impact 3D hero section, brief company overview, featured services, gallery preview, and clear calls-to-action.
- **About Us**: Company history (est. 2003), installation base, mission, and architectural values.
- **Services**: Detailed breakdown of elevator types, modernization, repairs, maintenance, and additional building services.
- **Gallery**: Portfolio of completed installations and product designs (cabins, doors, panels).
- **Contact Us**: Verified business phone numbers, email, location (Telangana & Andhra Pradesh), and a secure contact form.
