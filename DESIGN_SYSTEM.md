# Design System

## Creative Direction
- **Theme**: Premium architectural minimalism.
- **Imagery**: Cinematic elevator and building imagery, emphasizing realistic metal, brushed stainless steel, glass, dark stone, and architectural lighting.
- **Colors**:
  - Primary: Restrained electric-blue highlights (inspired by the logo).
  - Neutrals: White, graphite, deep navy.
  - Accents: Carefully controlled metallic accents. Orange and green used sparingly where appropriate to the brand.
- **Typography**: Strong, modern typography, with precise spacing, deliberate composition, and clear visual hierarchy.

## Motion & 3D Principles
- **3D Usage**: Signature elevator-inspired 3D hero, realistic brushed-metal and glass materials, layered architectural depth, subtle parallax, and elegant section transitions. Restricted to high-impact moments.
- **Fallbacks**: Provide static fallbacks for unsupported WebGL, reduced motion settings, and low-power devices.

## Responsive Rules & Accessibility
- **Mobile-first**: Start with small screens and scale up to large desktops.
- **Typography**: Readable at every viewport.
- **Interactions**: Touch-friendly controls, visible focus states, and keyboard-accessible navigation.
- **Contrast**: Sufficient contrast adhering to WCAG standards.
