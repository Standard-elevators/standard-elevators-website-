# Project Brief: Standard Engineering Works Elevators

## Business Scope
Standard Engineering Works Elevators provides elevator design, engineering solutions, new elevator installation, modernization, repairs, maintenance, and after-sales services. Additional services include structural fabrication, glass & ACP sheet works, UPVC windows & doors, renovation, SS railings, electrical house wiring, and civil works. Established in 2003 with an installation base of over 100 units.

## Audience
The primary audience includes:
- Building owners and developers seeking new elevator installations (commercial, residential, hospitals).
- Facility managers and building management seeking maintenance or modernization.
- Architects requiring specific aesthetic and structural solutions.

## Objectives
- Create a premium, distinctive, production-oriented website that feels like an engineering and architectural experience.
- Showcase products (MRL, Goods, Hospital, Hydraulic, Passenger elevators) and their technical specifications.
- Highlight customization options (cabins, doors, control panels).
- Generate leads through a well-designed, functional contact experience.
- Provide a secure, easy-to-use CMS for managing services and gallery images.

## Constraints
- The provided logo must not be altered, redrawn, or recolored.
- Information must strictly adhere to the provided brochure; facts must not be invented or hallucinated.
- 3D elements should be used purposefully and support fallback options for accessibility and performance.
- Mobile-first approach with full responsiveness and strict accessibility compliance.
